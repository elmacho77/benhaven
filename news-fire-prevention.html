Fire prevention in our schools

A community note based on the department’s public account of school visits. It does not name individual members.

Posted November 5, 2026 by Benhaven Emergency Services

October is Fire Prevention Month for fire departments across the country. Benhaven has used that month to visit schools, show children that firefighters are there to help, and teach them not to hide.

In a public account of one of those visits, crews went to Benhaven Elementary School. Students came out to meet them. Firefighters dressed in turnout gear and breathing apparatus so children could see and hear what a crew looks like on a call. After that short demonstration, the children looked over a fire truck, and some climbed inside to see the equipment.

The same account thanked the volunteers who came out that day, and said the department would not be what it is without them. No volunteer is named here.

The department has also described taking apparatus to other schools and day-care facilities for the same reason: children who have met a firefighter in gear are less likely to be afraid, and less likely to hide, if a crew ever comes to their home.

A calendar of upcoming class visits is not posted. A school that would like a visit can call 919-499-9511. Home habits for the rest of the year are on the community education page.
