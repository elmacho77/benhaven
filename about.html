About the department

Benhaven Emergency Services, also known as Benhaven Fire/Rescue, has used the motto Lucky 13 since its earlier public website.

Mission

Since 1982, the department has described its purpose in plain terms: protect people, property, and the environment by providing responsive, professional fire and rescue services essential to public health, safety, and well-being.

The mission of Benhaven Emergency Services is to protect people, property, and the environment with responsive, professional fire and emergency medical services. As a combination department, it brings together people, training, and equipment, strategically positioned to respond 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.

History

The public history of Benhaven Emergency Services begins in 1982. For years before that, the surrounding area needed a fire department and a rescue squad. Several attempts to vote one into being did not succeed.

  1. February 9, 1982 A meeting at Barbecue Presbyterian Church got the effort moving.
  2. March 26, 1982 Articles of incorporation were granted. The original committee became the board of directors. Names of those members are not published on this site.
  3. June 9, 1982 Harnett County commissioners held public hearings at Benhaven School and called referendums on a fire protection service district and an ambulance and rescue service district. This website does not publish vote totals.
  4. August 23, 1982 Ground was broken for both Station 1 (BBQ) and Station 2 (Olivia).
  5. Winter 1982 The department’s history says both buildings were virtually completed by winter.
  6. October 1, 1982 The service became operational.

Benhaven has operated since then as a paid and volunteer fire and EMS department. Members include people who are dual-certified in fire and EMS. A current headcount is not published here.

Where we serve

Benhaven serves the community around Olivia, Sanford, and Broadway, in the Harnett County and Lee County area. Stations stand at BBQ, Olivia, and Broadway. A mapped district boundary is not published on this site. For a specific address, call 911 in an emergency, or call 919-499-9511 with a non-emergency question. The 911 center sends the closest appropriate help.

The 1982 county hearings concerned Harnett County fire and rescue districts for this community. Station 3 in Broadway is operational today. This page does not treat the 1982 hearing boundary as a current map.

Organization

A combination department is led by chief officers and a board, and it is staffed by paid members and volunteers. Benhaven has not authorized a public roster, so this website lists titles only. See People for those titles and for what is intentionally left unpublished.

Staffing totals, insurance service ratings, and a drawn coverage map are not on this website. They will be added only if the department confirms them.
