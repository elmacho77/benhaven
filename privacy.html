Privacy

What this website collects, what it refuses to publish, and how to reach the department about either.

This note describes the public website. It is not a legal privacy policy drafted by counsel. Questions can go to 919-499-9511 or to info@bes13.com.

What we do not publish

Member, board, and leadership names and photos are not published until the department confirms a roster. Do not send personnel lists or identification photos through the contact form expecting them to be posted.

News and alerts are written without patient names, caller names, or private incident details.

Forms and email

The contact form and the join form are not connected to an online form service yet. Until a Formspree form ID is added to assets/js/config.js, submitting a form opens your email program and addresses the message to info@bes13.com. The message then travels through your email provider and, if it is delivered, to that inbox.

If the department later connects Formspree, submissions will be sent to Formspree and forwarded to the address the department chooses there. Formspree’s own privacy terms will apply to that delivery. The form will say, on the page, when that change is made.

Do not send medical information, Social Security numbers, or copies of identity documents through either form.

Calls and maps

Phone links open your phone’s dialer. The call is handled by your carrier, not by this website. Map buttons leave this site and open Google Maps or OpenStreetMap, which have their own privacy practices.

Cookies and hosting

This website does not set analytics cookies and does not run advertising. It is hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep ordinary server logs of requests to the site under GitHub’s own terms.

Children

The junior firefighter note is information for families. The website does not ask children to create accounts or to send personal details.
