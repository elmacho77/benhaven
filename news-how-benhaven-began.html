How Benhaven began

A history note drawn from the department’s public account and from county records of the 1982 hearings.

Posted November 5, 2026 by Benhaven Emergency Services

This note was posted with the new website. The dates in it are from the public record, not from a new incident.

People in this community had tried to create a fire department and a rescue squad before 1982. Those votes did not pass. On February 9, 1982, a meeting at Barbecue Presbyterian Church started the effort that did.

Articles of incorporation were granted on March 26, 1982, and the original committee became the board of directors. This website does not list those names.

On June 9, 1982, the Harnett County Board of Commissioners met at Benhaven School for public hearings on a fire protection service district and an ambulance and rescue service district, and called referendums on both. Vote totals are not published here.

August 23, 1982, was groundbreaking day for Station 1 at BBQ and Station 2 at Olivia. The department’s history says both buildings were virtually completed by winter. On October 1, 1982, Benhaven Emergency Services became operational.

It has remained a combination department: paid members and volunteers, fire and EMS, including dual-certified members, in service to this community. The longer version of this story, and what the site deliberately leaves out, is on the About page.
