Ask about volunteering, a junior firefighter seat, or paid work. This page does not invent openings or requirements.

Volunteer fire

Benhaven has served since 1982 with volunteers alongside paid members. If you want to ask about the fire side of the department, call Station 2’s non-emergency line before you visit, or use the form on this page.

Volunteer EMS and support

EMS has been part of the department from the start. Some members are dual-certified in fire and EMS. Support help, including public-education events, has also been part of the department’s public story. Ask which of those roles are active now. This page will not guess.

Paid openings are not posted yet. The department’s earlier website said current job opportunities and volunteer applications could be obtained at Station 2 in Olivia. Call 919-499-9511 before you go, and use the form if you want the department to call you back.

Requirements

Benhaven has not published a current requirement list on this website. The department will tell you what each role requires. Do not rely on a generic list from another agency.

  • A junior firefighter program has been publicly described for participants who are at least 16 and interested in a firefighting career.
  • Volunteer applications were previously available at Station 2, 4023 Hwy 87 N, Sanford / Olivia. Call first.
  • Age, driver’s license, training, certification, and background requirements will be confirmed by the department for the role you ask about.

Training rhythm to confirm

An earlier public post described a regular calendar. Treat it as historical until the department confirms it:

  • Fire training on the second Monday of the month
  • A general meeting on the fourth Tuesday
  • EMS continuing education each month, on dates that varied

Call 919-499-9511 before you plan to attend.

Questions people ask

Do I need a certification before I ask?

Ask the department. Some roles may train you. Some may expect a certification already. That list is not published here, so the form and the phone are the right first step.

Can I volunteer only for EMS, or only in a support role?

The department has publicly included both fire and EMS, plus community support work. Which of those you can join now is a question for Benhaven. Say what you are offering when you call or write.

Will my name be posted on this website?

No. Names and photos stay off the site until the department authorizes a roster. Sending this form does not put you on a public page.

Is there a paid job open right now?

No paid opening is posted on this website. Call the non-emergency number or send the form to ask. If openings are confirmed later, they will be listed here.

Tell the department you are interested

This form is not connected to an online inbox yet. Submitting it opens your email app with the message filled in, addressed to info@bes13.com. You can also call 919-499-9511 or visit Station 2 after you call.

Do not include medical details or identity documents. Names sent here are not published on the website. See the privacy note.
