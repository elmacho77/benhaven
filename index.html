Our mission

Protect people, property, and the environment

The mission of Benhaven Emergency Services is to protect people, property, and the environment by providing responsive, professional fire and emergency medical services essential to public health, safety, and well-being.

Benhaven is a combination department. Paid members and volunteers, including members certified in both fire and EMS, are strategically positioned to respond 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.
