Contact

Emergencies go to 911. Everything else on this page can wait.

If this is an emergency, dial 911. Do not use this form, the email, or the station phone for a fire, a medical emergency, or a rescue.

Email

This form is not connected to an online inbox yet. Submitting it opens your email app with the message filled in, addressed to benhavenfire@charter.net. You can also call 919-499-9511.
