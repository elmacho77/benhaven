Paid members and volunteers

Benhaven is a combination department. Paid staff and volunteers serve together in fire and EMS. Some members are dual-certified and can work in both disciplines. Support roles have been part of the department’s public description of community work. Which support roles are open is a question for the department, not a list this website invents.

A junior firefighter program has been publicly described for youth who are at least 16 and interested in a firefighting career. Age, training, and other requirements for every role will be confirmed by the department. See Join Us.
